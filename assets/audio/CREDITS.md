# Recorded ambience

Quiet, edited field-recording loops, mixed by the camera's distance from rivers, trees and settlements. These recordings illustrate a landscape; they are not recordings of the Mahaweli or a local bird-species survey.

The excerpts, equalization and seamless-loop processing are from [Todak's distributed ambience assets and credits](https://github.com/euuuuuuan/todak-public/blob/main/CREDITS.md). The stream is credited here under its original source's current CC BY license. Riverkeeper applies additional volume changes and low-pass filtering for quiet background use. All four files are served locally; playback makes no third-party requests.

Passing-weather rain and thunder are original procedural Web Audio effects, generated locally by `weather-audio.mjs`. Soft filtered noise supplies rain; a shaped low-frequency noise envelope supplies rolling thunder. They are not field recordings. Thunder is delayed and panned relative to the current view, with a compressed 1.2–9 second delay for gameplay. Weather effects share the speaker mute, volume control and compressor. Muting or hiding the tab cancels pending thunder.
