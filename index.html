Scoring method & model status

Every completed month adds service and basin-health benefits, then subtracts harm and resilience penalties. Damaging months can reduce the trophy total below zero. Placement previews show the change in expected monthly points; construction itself awards nothing.

Stewardship offers up to 100 points and balanced development up to 750. Cash itself earns no points. During scheduled events, service reliability and environmental protection add up to 40 points; failures subtract up to 120. Shortages, pollution, ecological-flow stress and other harms also carry the normal monthly penalties. Points can be negative. All weights and scenario timings are educational game-design choices. The separate Mahaweli SWAT+ baseline is not connected to these scores.
